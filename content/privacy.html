Privacy

Quiren is software that runs on your machine. We do not run a service that receives your memory.

What the software stores

Notes, transcripts, wiki pages, proposals and their history are plain files under ~/.quiren (or QUIREN_HOME), one folder per project. Known secret patterns are replaced with [REDACTED:kind] markers before anything is written. This catches known patterns, not everything.

What leaves your machine

Nothing, until you dream. A dream runs on a model on your machine (Ollama or LM Studio) and sends nothing anywhere. If you choose NVIDIA's hosted models (QUIREN_LLM_PROVIDER=nvidia), the new transcripts, notes and wiki pages of that dream go to NVIDIA, under your agreement with them, and nowhere else. A dream through your agent (dream_prepare / dream_submit) hands the same material to that agent, which talks to its own provider. There is no telemetry and no account.

This website

The site is static pages. The simulation at /try runs in your browser in recorded mode: no model is called and nothing you click is sent or saved. Your hosting provider may keep standard server logs (IP address, time, page requested).

Deleting your data

Delete the project folder under ~/.quiren/spaces/, or the whole ~/.quiren folder. quiren retention removes old transcripts and history automatically.
